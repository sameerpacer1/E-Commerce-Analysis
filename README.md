# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis

📌 **Project Description**  
This project analyzes sales data from the Sample Superstore dataset using Python & Jupyter Notebook.  
It explores customer behavior, product categories, and regional sales performance with interactive Plotly visualizations.  

---

🚀 **Features**  
- Customer & product insights  
- Regional sales breakdown  
- Shipping mode comparison  
- Interactive charts with Plotly  

---

📂 **Dataset**  
The dataset (`Sample - Superstore.csv`) contains order-level details including:  
- Customer information  
- Product categories & sub-categories  
- Order & shipping details  
- Regional sales data  

---

📝 **Analysis Tasks**  
The following tasks were performed to derive insights from the dataset:  
1. Calculate monthly sales and identify the highest and lowest sales months.  
2. Analyze sales by product categories to determine the best and worst performing categories.  
3. Perform sales analysis based on sub-categories.  
4. Calculate monthly profit and identify the most and least profitable months.  
5. Analyze profit by category and sub-category.  
6. Study sales and profit distribution across customer segments.  
7. Evaluate the sales-to-profit ratio for overall business performance.  

---

📊 **Key Insights from Analysis**  
- **Sales Trends**: November emerged as the peak month for sales, while January recorded the lowest activity.  
- **Category Performance**: Technology dominates both in total sales and profitability. Within sub-categories, phones are the highest sellers, while copiers generate the most profit.  
- **Profitability**: December was the most profitable month, whereas January saw the lowest profits. Tables were identified as a product category causing financial loss.  
- **Customer Segments**: Consumers are the primary drivers of both sales volume and profit, followed by Corporate and Home Office segments.  
- **Strategic Value**: Analyzing the relationship between sales and profit is essential for businesses to optimize product inventory and focus marketing efforts effectively.
